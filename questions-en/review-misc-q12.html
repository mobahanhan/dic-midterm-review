Concepts You Need

A CMOS\mathrm{CMOS}CMOS inverter uses a PMOS\mathrm{PMOS}PMOS pull-up to the supply and an NMOS\mathrm{NMOS}NMOS pull-down to ground. The voltage transfer characteristic (VTC\mathrm{VTC}VTC) gives the static input–output relationship. The switching threshold VMV_MVM​ is the voltage at which input equals output. The slope g=dVoutdVing=\frac{dV_{out}}{dV_{in}}g=dVin​dVout​​ is the gain. VOH,VOLV_{OH},V_{OL}VOH​,VOL​ are the high and low output voltages, and VIL,VIHV_{IL},V_{IH}VIL​,VIH​ are the low and high input thresholds. The noise margins are NML=VIL−VOL\mathrm{NM}_{L}=V_{IL}-V_{OL}NML​=VIL​−VOL​ and NMH=VOH−VIH\mathrm{NM}_{H}=V_{OH}-V_{IH}NMH​=VOH​−VIH​.
