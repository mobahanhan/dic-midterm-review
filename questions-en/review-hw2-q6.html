Concepts You Need

A MOS\mathrm{MOS}MOS field-effect transistor uses its gate GGG to control the channel between the source SSS and drain DDD. The body is denoted by BBB. NMOS\mathrm{NMOS}NMOS conducts via electrons and usually has its source at the lower potential. PMOS\mathrm{PMOS}PMOS conducts via holes and usually has its source at the higher potential. A two-terminal voltage is the first terminal's potential minus the second's, as in VGS=VG−VSV_{\mathrm{GS}}=V_G-V_SVGS​=VG​−VS​. The threshold VTV_TVT​ marks turn-on.
