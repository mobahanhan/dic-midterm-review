Concepts You Need

A pseudo-NMOS\mathrm{NMOS}NMOS inverter uses a PMOS\mathrm{PMOS}PMOS with its gate grounded as the always-on pull-up. The input controls the NMOS\mathrm{NMOS}NMOS pull-down. When the output is low, both devices conduct. The low output VOLV_{OL}VOL​ is usually above zero and there is static current. The high output VOHV_{OH}VOH​ is close to the supply. Since the output quality depends on the strength ratio, this is ratioed logic. VMV_MVM​ is the switching point at which input equals output, and VIL,VIHV_{IL},V_{IH}VIL​,VIH​ can be found at the two points where the voltage transfer curve has slope minus one.
