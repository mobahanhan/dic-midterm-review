Concepts You Need

The load CLC_LCL​ is the total capacitance the output must charge or discharge. It includes the driver's intrinsic output capacitance CintC_{\mathrm{int}}Cint​ and external capacitance CextC_{\mathrm{ext}}Cext​, such as the following gates and interconnect. The conducting device is modeled by an equivalent resistance ReqR_{\mathrm{eq}}Req​. In a first-order model, the output reaches half the supply voltage after about ln⁡2 ReqCL≈0.69ReqCL\ln2\,R_{\mathrm{eq}}C_L\approx0.69R_{\mathrm{eq}}C_Lln2Req​CL​≈0.69Req​CL​. tpHL,tpLHt_{pHL},t_{pLH}tpHL​,tpLH​ refer to falling and rising outputs, respectively, and their mean is the average delay. The gate input capacitance is CgC_gCg​. Electrical fan-out is the ratio of external load capacitance to this input capacitance. Use the specified model if its fitted parameters already include the numerical delay factor.
