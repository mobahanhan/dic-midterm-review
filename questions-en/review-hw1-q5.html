Concepts You Need

A wafer is a round silicon substrate used to fabricate many circuits at once. An individual circuit cut from it is a die. The yield YYY is the fraction of dies that pass testing and is used as a decimal. If NdieN_{die}Ndie​ is the number of dies per wafer and CwaferC_{wafer}Cwafer​ the wafer cost, the cost per good die is Cwafer/(NdieY)C_{wafer}/(N_{die}Y)Cwafer​/(Ndie​Y). Account for incomplete dies at the wafer edge in a geometric estimate.
