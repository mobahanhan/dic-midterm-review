Concepts You Need

Parasitic capacitance is an inherent part of the device structure and must be charged and discharged. The gate overlaps the source and drain edges, producing overlap capacitance. The interaction of the gate and channel charge produces channel capacitance. The source and drain diffusions form PN\mathrm{PN}PN junctions with the body and thus have junction capacitance. Cgs,Cgd,CgbC_{gs},C_{gd},C_{gb}Cgs​,Cgd​,Cgb​ connect the gate to the source, drain, and body, respectively. Cdb,CsbC_{db},C_{sb}Cdb​,Csb​ connect the drain and source to the body, respectively. CoxC_{\mathrm{ox}}Cox​ is the gate-oxide capacitance per unit area, and W,L\mathrm{W},LW,L are the channel width and length. The junction bottom area is A\mathrm{A}A, and the sidewall perimeter is PPP. Multiply the bottom capacitance per unit area by A\mathrm{A}A, and multiply the sidewall capacitance per unit length by the corresponding perimeter. When input and output move in opposite directions by equal amounts, the capacitance between them has about twice its nominal value when referred to either node. This is the Miller effect. Question

Which capacitance component of the diffusion area is proportional to the bottom area WHD\mathrm{W} H_DWHD​?

  1. A. sidewall capacitance
  2. B. base plate junction capacitance
  3. C. overlapping capacitance
  4. D. gate capacitance
Show Answer and Explanation

Answer and Explanation

Choose BBB. The bottom junction capacitance is equal to the unit area junction capacitance multiplied area; The sidewall items are calculated according to the circumference, and the overlapping items are calculated according to the width.
