Concepts You Need

Propagation delay is the time from an input transition to the corresponding output transition. It is usually measured between their crossings of half the supply voltage. The direction refers to the output: tpLHt_{pLH}tpLH​ is low-to-high and tpHLt_{pHL}tpHL​ is high-to-low. Rise time trt_rtr​ is usually measured from 10%10\%10% to 90%90\%90%, and fall time tft_ftf​ from 90%90\%90% to 10%10\%10%. The tpHLt_{pHL}tpHL​ defines the response time of the gate for between

  1. A. 50 %50\,\%50% high to low input swing and low to high 50 %50\,\%50% output swing
  2. B. 50 %50\,\%50% input swing and high to low 50 %50\,\%50% output swing
  3. C. 50 %50\,\%50% high to low input swing and 50 %50\,\%50% output swing
  4. D. 50 %50\,\%50% low to high input swing and 50 %50\,\%50% output swing
Show Answer and Explanation

B. The suffix HL refers to the output going from high to low. Measure from the input's triggering midpoint crossing to the output's falling midpoint crossing.
