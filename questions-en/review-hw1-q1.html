Concepts You Need

An inverter produces a high output for a low input and a low output for a high input. Its static input–output relationship is the voltage transfer characteristic (VTC\mathrm{VTC}VTC), plotted with input on the horizontal axis. VDDV_{\mathrm{DD}}VDD​ is the supply voltage. Question

What does ideal inverter mean? (multiple choice)

  1. A. The output resistance is too large
  2. B. Input resistance is too large
  3. C. Output resistance is too small
  4. D. Input resistance is too small
  5. E. low level noise margin too large
  6. F. low level noise margin too small
  7. G. The absolute value of the gain is large
  8. H. The absolute value of the gain is small
Show Answer and Explanation

Answer and Explanation

Answer: BBB, CCC, GGG.

The input resistance measures the DC load affected by the front stage, and the output resistance measures the degree that the output voltage of this stage is affected by the load; Gain is the rate of change of the output voltage relative to the input voltage. The ideal voltage logic gate expects that the input will hardly absorb current, the output will keep the level, and the flip area is steep, so:

Ri→∞,Ro→0,∣dVoutdVin∣→∞.R_i\to\infty,\qquad R_o\to0,\qquad \left|\frac{dV_{out}}{dV_{in}}\right|\to\infty.Ri​→∞,Ro​→0,​dVin​dVout​​​→∞.

Noise margin represents the noise amplitude that the logic level can tolerate. Switching Threshold's ideal symmetry inverter is located at the midpoint of the power supply:

NML=NMH=VDD2.\mathrm{NM}_{L}=\mathrm{NM}_{H}=\frac{V_{\mathrm{DD}}}{2}.NML​=NMH​=2VDD​​.

The "oversized" wording in the option EEE is ambiguous: Noise margin is usually the advantage. The source answer is not selected for EEE, and this question should be retained. It cannot be explained as "big noise margin is not ideal".
