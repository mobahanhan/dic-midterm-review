Concepts You Need

Propagation delay is the time from an input transition to the corresponding output transition. It is usually measured between their crossings of half the supply voltage. The direction refers to the output: tpLHt_{pLH}tpLH​ is low-to-high and tpHLt_{pHL}tpHL​ is high-to-low. Rise time trt_rtr​ is usually measured from 10%10\%10% to 90%90\%90%, and fall time tft_ftf​ from 90%90\%90% to 10%10\%10%.
