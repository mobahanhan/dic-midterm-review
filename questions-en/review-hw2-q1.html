Concepts You Need

Doping changes the carrier concentration by introducing impurities. Holes are the majority carriers in the PPP region, and electrons are the majority carriers in the NNN region. The interface forms a PN\mathrm{PN}PN junction. The depletion layer near the junction has fewer mobile carriers. The junction is forward biased when the PPP side is at a higher potential than the NNN side, and reverse biased for the opposite polarity. A larger reverse bias widens the depletion layer and lowers junction capacitance: Cj=Cj0(1+VR/ϕ0)−mC_j=C_{j0}(1+V_R/\phi_0)^{-\mathrm{m}}Cj​=Cj0​(1+VR​/ϕ0​)−m. Here VRV_RVR​ is the reverse-bias magnitude, Cj0C_{j0}Cj0​ the zero-bias capacitance, ϕ0\phi_0ϕ0​ the built-in potential parameter, and m\mathrm{m}m the grading coefficient. Under forward bias, stored minority carriers also give rise to diffusion capacitance.

Answer and Explanation

Answer: CCC, forward bias, NA=1014 cm−3N_\mathrm{A}=10^{14}\,\mathrm{\mathrm{cm}^{-3}}NA​=1014cm−3.

Most carriers are carriers that dominate in a certain area, while minority carriers are the opposite. Most of the carriers in the PPP area are holes, and the platform concentration of the distance away from the junction is approximately equal to the doping concentration of the subject, so read the figure:

NA≈pP,far=1014 cm−3.N_\mathrm{A}\approx p_{P,\mathrm{far}}=10^{14}\,\mathrm{\mathrm{cm}^{-3}}.NA​≈pP,far​=1014cm−3.

Most of the carriers in the NNN area are electrons, and the platform on the right corresponds to ND≈1016 cm−3N_D\approx10^{16}\,\mathrm{\mathrm{cm}^{-3}}ND​≈1016cm−3, which cannot be regarded as NAN_\mathrm{A}NA​.

Forward bias will inject a minority carrier into both sides, so that the concentration of the minority carrier at the junction boundary is higher than the distal equilibrium value; Reverse bias reduces the concentration of minority carriers at the junction boundary. The figure belongs to the former situation, so it is forward bias.

The original image is written as −xn-x_n−xn​ and the right border as xpx_pxp​, and the common PPP side −xp-x_p−xp​ and NNN side xnx_nxn​ marks are different; This is the problem of the original figure marking, which does not affect the reading offset and concentration of this question.
