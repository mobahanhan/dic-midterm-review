Concepts You Need

A wafer is a round silicon substrate used to fabricate many circuits at once. An individual circuit cut from it is a die. The yield YYY is the fraction of dies that pass testing and is used as a decimal. If NdieN_{die}Ndie​ is the number of dies per wafer and CwaferC_{wafer}Cwafer​ the wafer cost, the cost per good die is Cwafer/(NdieY)C_{wafer}/(N_{die}Y)Cwafer​/(Ndie​Y). Account for incomplete dies at the wafer edge in a geometric estimate.

Newly written practice

A wafer costs 6,000 yuan to process, yields up to 400 dies, and has a good-die yield of 75%75\%75%. Packaging and testing cost 3 yuan per good die. Find the cost per finished device. Repeat for a yield of 60%60\%60%.

Show Answer and Explanation

At 75%75\%75% yield there are 300 good dies, so each costs 6000/300+3=236000/300+3=236000/300+3=23 yuan. At 60%60\%60% yield there are 240 good dies and the cost is 6000/240+3=286000/240+3=286000/240+3=28 yuan.
