Concepts You Need

A MOS\mathrm{MOS}MOS field-effect transistor uses its gate GGG to control the channel between the source SSS and drain DDD. The body is denoted by BBB. NMOS\mathrm{NMOS}NMOS conducts via electrons and usually has its source at the lower potential. PMOS\mathrm{PMOS}PMOS conducts via holes and usually has its source at the higher potential. A two-terminal voltage is the first terminal's potential minus the second's, as in VGS=VG−VSV_{\mathrm{GS}}=V_G-V_SVGS​=VG​−VS​. The threshold VTV_TVT​ marks turn-on. The NMOS\mathrm{NMOS}NMOS overdrive is vov=VGS−VTv_{\mathrm{ov}}=V_{\mathrm{GS}}-V_Tvov​=VGS​−VT​. Above threshold it is in the linear region when the drain-to-source voltage magnitude is below the overdrive, and in pinch-off saturation when it reaches the overdrive. In K=μCoxW/LK=\mu C_{\mathrm{ox}}\mathrm{W}/LK=μCox​W/L, μ\muμ is mobility, CoxC_{\mathrm{ox}}Cox​ is gate-oxide capacitance per unit area, and W,L\mathrm{W},LW,L are channel width and length.
