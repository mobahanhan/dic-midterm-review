Concepts You Need

Doping changes the carrier concentration by introducing impurities. Holes are the majority carriers in the PPP region, and electrons are the majority carriers in the NNN region. The interface forms a PN\mathrm{PN}PN junction. The depletion layer near the junction has fewer mobile carriers. The junction is forward biased when the PPP side is at a higher potential than the NNN side, and reverse biased for the opposite polarity. A larger reverse bias widens the depletion layer and lowers junction capacitance: Cj=Cj0(1+VR/ϕ0)−mC_j=C_{j0}(1+V_R/\phi_0)^{-\mathrm{m}}Cj​=Cj0​(1+VR​/ϕ0​)−m. Here VRV_RVR​ is the reverse-bias magnitude, Cj0C_{j0}Cj0​ the zero-bias capacitance, ϕ0\phi_0ϕ0​ the built-in potential parameter, and m\mathrm{m}m the grading coefficient. Under forward bias, stored minority carriers also give rise to diffusion capacitance.

Question

When the reverse bias voltage of the PN\mathrm{PN}PN junction increases, how does its Depletion Region width and Junction Capacitance change?

  1. A. increase, decrease
  2. B. increase, increase
  3. C. decrease, increase
  4. D. decrease, decrease
Show Answer and Explanation

Answer and Explanation

Answer: AAA, the width of the Depletion Region increases, and the Junction Capacitance decreases.

Depletion region is a space charge region where free carriers are missing near the PN\mathrm{PN}PN junction. Depletion region widens when Reverse Bias is enhanced; From a capacitance perspective, this corresponds to an increase in the spacing of the plates, so the capacitance is reduced.

If the magnitude of the voltage of Reverse Bias is denoted as VR>0V_R>0VR​>0, then:

Wdep∝ϕ0+VR,Cj≈εAWdep.W_{dep}\propto\sqrt{\phi_0+V_R},\qquad C_j\approx\frac{\varepsilon \mathrm{A}}{W_{dep}}.Wdep​∝ϕ0​+VR​​,Cj​≈Wdep​εA​.

The common junction capacitance expression is:

Cj(VR)=Cj0(1+VRϕ0)−m.C_j(V_R)=C_{j0}\left(1+\frac{V_R}{\phi_0}\right)^{-\mathrm{m}}.Cj​(VR​)=Cj0​(1+ϕ0​VR​​)−m.

Among them, Cj0C_{j0}Cj0​ is Zero Bias Junction Capacitance, ϕ0\phi_0ϕ0​ is the built-in potential, and m\mathrm{m}m is the knot gradient coefficient. As VRV_RVR​ increases, CjC_jCj​ decreases. If the diode signed voltage VD=VP−VNV_D=V_P-V_NVD​=VP​−VN​ is used instead, the Reverse Bias enhancement corresponds to the VDV_DVD​ and becomes more negative, and pay attention to the symbol convention.
