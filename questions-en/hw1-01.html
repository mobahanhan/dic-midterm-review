Concepts You Need

An inverter produces a high output for a low input and a low output for a high input. Its static input–output relationship is the voltage transfer characteristic (VTC\mathrm{VTC}VTC), plotted with input on the horizontal axis. VDDV_{\mathrm{DD}}VDD​ is the supply voltage. VOH,VOLV_{OH},V_{OL}VOH​,VOL​ are the minimum guaranteed high output and maximum guaranteed low output, respectively. VIH,VILV_{IH},V_{IL}VIH​,VIL​ are the input thresholds for a guaranteed high and low, respectively. The noise margins quantify the allowable voltage disturbance: NMH=VOH−VIH,NML=VIL−VOL\mathrm{NM}_{H}=V_{OH}-V_{IH},\mathrm{NM}_{L}=V_{IL}-V_{OL}NMH​=VOH​−VIH​,NML​=VIL​−VOL​. The local slope g=dVoutdVing=\frac{dV_{out}}{dV_{in}}g=dVin​dVout​​ is the gain. On a flat portion of the curve, ∣g∣<1|g|<1∣g∣<1 attenuates small deviations, which allows logic-level regeneration. The input voltage at which input and output are equal is the switching threshold VMV_MVM​.

Original English question

An inverter has VOHV_{OH}VOH​ = 1.10 V1.10\,\mathrm{V}1.10V, VOLV_{OL}VOL​ = 0.10 V0.10\,\mathrm{V}0.10V, VIHV_{IH}VIH​ = 0.80 V0.80\,\mathrm{V}0.80V, and VILV_{IL}VIL​ = 0.30 V0.30\,\mathrm{V}0.30V. What are its high- and low-level noise margins (NMHNM_HNMH​, NMLNM_LNML​), respectively?

  1. A. 0.30 V0.30\,\mathrm{V}0.30V, 0.20 V0.20\,\mathrm{V}0.20V
  2. B. 0.80 V0.80\,\mathrm{V}0.80V, 0.30 V0.30\,\mathrm{V}0.30V
  3. C. 0.20 V0.20\,\mathrm{V}0.20V, 0.30 V0.30\,\mathrm{V}0.30V
  4. D. 1.00 V1.00\,\mathrm{V}1.00V, 0.20 V0.20\,\mathrm{V}0.20V
Show Answer and Explanation

A. Subtract the receiver's high threshold from the driver's high output: NMH=1.10−0.80=0.30 VNM_H=1.10-0.80=0.30\,\mathrm{V}NMH​=1.10−0.80=0.30V. Subtract the driver's low output from the receiver's low threshold: NML=0.30−0.10=0.20 VNM_L=0.30-0.10=0.20\,\mathrm{V}NML​=0.30−0.10=0.20V.
