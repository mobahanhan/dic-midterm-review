English rendering of the Chinese source

Explain the trade-off between noise margin and delay in pseudo-NMOS logic.

Show Answer and Explanation

A stronger always-on PMOS charges the load faster, but raises the low output by opposing the NMOS pull-down. That generally hurts low-level noise margin and raises static current. A weaker pull-up improves the low level and reduces contention while slowing the rising output. Compare both margins and both edge delays under the same load.
