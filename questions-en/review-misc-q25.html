Concepts You Need

Channel-length modulation is the shortening of the effective channel in saturation as drain voltage rises. Velocity saturation occurs when a high lateral electric field pushes carrier velocity close to its limit. Drain-induced barrier lowering (DIBL\mathrm{DIBL}DIBL) occurs when the drain field lowers the source barrier and reduces the effective threshold. Subthreshold current is the small current that flows below threshold. Temperature, fabrication variation, and the supply voltage affect mobility, threshold voltage, leakage, and delay. A process corner models a particular combination of fast or slow devices. Each effect describes a different mechanism.
