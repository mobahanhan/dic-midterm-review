Concepts You Need

Channel-length modulation is the shortening of the effective channel in saturation as drain voltage rises. Velocity saturation occurs when a high lateral electric field pushes carrier velocity close to its limit. Drain-induced barrier lowering (DIBL\mathrm{DIBL}DIBL) occurs when the drain field lowers the source barrier and reduces the effective threshold. Subthreshold current is the small current that flows below threshold. Temperature, fabrication variation, and the supply voltage affect mobility, threshold voltage, leakage, and delay. A process corner models a particular combination of fast or slow devices. Each effect describes a different mechanism.

Question

What factors usually increase the leakage of subthresholds? Ⅰ The threshold is reduced; Ⅱ Power supply is improved; Ⅲ Temperature increase; IV DRAIN-TO-SOURCE VOLTAGE Improved.

  1. A. Ⅰ、Ⅱ
  2. B. Ⅰ、Ⅱ、Ⅲ
  3. C. Ⅰ、Ⅱ、Ⅳ
  4. D. Ⅰ、Ⅱ、Ⅲ、Ⅳ
Show Answer and Explanation

Answer and Explanation

Choose DDD. Thresholds lower the turn-on threshold in the direct reduction index; Temperature changes the thermal voltage and threshold; Increased drain voltage and associated Drain-Induced Barrier Lowering can increase the shutdown current. The power supply here is to improve the understanding of the drain terminal voltage of the actual shut-off transistor with the power supply, and it is not an independent parameter that is independent of the terminal voltage in any ideal model.
