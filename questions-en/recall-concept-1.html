Draw the voltage transfer characteristics of an unskewed CMOS inverter and a high-skew CMOS inverter. Compare their switching thresholds VMV_MVM​.

Show Answer and Explanation

The high-skew inverter has a stronger pull-up relative to its pull-down. It needs a higher input voltage to balance the two currents, so its VTC moves right and its VMV_MVM​ rises compared with the unskewed design. The plot shows the shift. A balanced symmetric inverter switches near VDD/2V_{DD}/2VDD​/2.
