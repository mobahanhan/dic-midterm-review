Answer and Explanation

The VTC\mathrm{VTC}VTC of the high skew inverter moves to the right, and its switching threshold is usually higher than the unskewed inverter.

Unskewed means that the pull-up and pull-down drivers are balanced, and the geometric widths of the PMOS\mathrm{PMOS}PMOS and NMOS\mathrm{NMOS}NMOS must be the same. High skew preferentially improves the flip of the output from low to high, usually by reducing the relative strength of the NMOS\mathrm{NMOS}NMOS and making the PMOS\mathrm{PMOS}PMOS relatively stronger. Higher input is required to balance the pull-down and pull-up current, so VMV_MVM​ is enlarged.
