Question

The FAN-OUT of the NNN-level chain is the same as fff, f≫1f\gg1f≫1 and ignores the inherent delay of each level. What is the total delay approximated?

  1. A. NfNfNf
  2. B. N+fN+fN+f
  3. C. fNf^NfN
  4. D. N/fN/fN/f
Show Answer and Explanation

Answer and Explanation

Choose AAA: NfNfNf. The load items of each level of delay are proportional to their own fan-out, and NNN are added to the same contribution. fNf^NfN is the total load multiple, not chain delay.
