互连层 · interconnect layer

芯片中用来连接器件和电路节点的一层导线。不同金属层之间用通孔连接。导线的电阻和电容会影响信号到达时间，也可能在相邻线之间传递干扰。

An interconnect layer routes signals and power between circuit nodes. Vias connect different metal layers. Wire resistance and capacitance affect timing and coupling between neighboring routes.

铜阻挡层 · copper diffusion barrier

位于铜导线与周围材料之间的薄层，用于抑制铜向绝缘层或半导体中扩散。在给定线宽下，它会占用一部分可供铜导电的截面。

AAA copper diffusion barrier is aaa thin layer that prevents copper from migrating into surrounding dielectrics or semiconductor material. It occupies part of the conductor cross-section at aaa given linewidth.

凹陷效应 · CMP dishing

在化学机械抛光（CMP）过程中，金属线中央被磨得比周围绝缘表面更低，形成浅凹面。铜的有效厚度减小，导线电阻可能增大。

Dishing occurs when chemical mechanical polishing (CMP) leaves the metal surface recessed relative to its surroundings. The loss of effective thickness can increase wire resistance.

趋肤效应 · skin effect

高频交变电流在导体内更集中于表面，内部参与导电的程度降低。频率越高，电流集中的表层通常越薄，导线的交流电阻随之增大。

At high frequency, alternating current crowds near aaa conductor'sss surface. The effective conducting depth decreases as frequency rises, increasing AC\mathrm{AC}AC resistance.

串扰 · crosstalk

一根线变化的信号通过相邻线间的电场或磁场影响另一根线。芯片导线题常分析耦合电容造成的电压毛刺和延时变化。两线同向与反向切换时，耦合电容的相对电压变化不同。

Crosstalk is the disturbance induced by aaa switching wire on aaa nearby wire through electric or magnetic coupling. On-chip problems often focus on capacitive noise and delay changes. Same-direction and opposite-direction transitions impose different swings across the coupling capacitance.

集总 RC 模型 · lumped RC model

用有限个电阻和电容元件近似整根导线，例如将总电阻集中为一段、总电容集中到一个节点。RC 指电阻和电容。模型简单，适合粗估，精度取决于导线长度和所选结构。

AAA lumped RC model represents aaa wire with aaa finite set of resistors and capacitors. Concentrating the wire'sss total resistance and capacitance at aaa few points gives aaa simple approximation whose accuracy depends on the wire and model topology.

分布式 RC 模型 · distributed RC model

把导线电阻和电容视为沿长度连续分布。可把线切成许多短段，每段串联一小段电阻并带对地电容。这样能描述不同位置的电压逐步到达和波形变化。

AAA distributed RC model spreads resistance and capacitance along the wire. AAA ladder of short sections, each with series resistance and shunt capacitance, approximates the voltage waveform at different positions.
