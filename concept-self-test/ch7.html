DIC · 中英术语与释义

半加器 · half adder

把两个一位二进制输入 A\mathrm{A}A 和 BBB 相加，输出和位 SSS 与进位 CCC。S=A⊕BS=\mathrm{A}\oplus BS=A⊕B，C=ABC=ABC=AB。符号 ⊕\oplus⊕ 表示异或，即恰有一个输入为 111 时输出 111。并写 ABABAB 表示两个输入都为 111。

AAA half adder adds two one-bit inputs. Its sum is the exclusive OR, which is high when exactly one input is high. Its carry is their AND, high when both inputs are high.

全加器 · full adder

把两个一位数 A\mathrm{A}A、BBB 和低位传来的进位 CinC_{in}Cin​ 相加，输出本位和与向高位的进位。三个输入中有奇数个 111 时和位为 111，至少两个为 111 时进位为 111。

AAA full adder adds two one-bit operands and aaa carry-in. The sum is high for an odd number of high inputs, and the carry-out is high when at least two inputs are high.

清单来源《DIC 概念题自测》第 2 页

镜像特性 · mirror-adder symmetry查看释义收起释义

镜像特性 · mirror-adder symmetry

对一位全加器，同时反转三个输入，和位与进位也都反转。镜像加法器利用这种对称性，用相似结构组织上拉与下拉网络。这里的镜像指逻辑关系与对应的电路结构。

Complementing all three inputs of aaa full adder complements both sum and carry-out. AAA mirror adder exploits this symmetry to arrange its pull-up and pull-down networks in corresponding structures.

消除 · carry kill (delete)查看释义收起释义

消除 · carry kill (delete)

一位加法器的两个操作数都是 000 时，输出进位固定为 000。即使低位传来进位 111，也不能传到下一位。这种状态叫进位消除。

AAA bit kills the incoming carry when both operand bits are zero. The carry-out is then zero regardless of the carry-in.

传播 · carry propagate查看释义收起释义

传播 · carry propagate

当两个操作数恰有一个为 111，该位会把输入进位原样传到下一位。常用传播信号 P=A⊕BP=\mathrm{A}\oplus BP=A⊕B 表示这种情况。有些进位公式改用 P=A+BP=\mathrm{A}+BP=A+B，这里的加号表示逻辑或，要按题目约定。

When exactly one operand bit is high, the bit passes the carry-in through to the carry-out. XOR is aaa common propagate definition. Some carry equations use OR instead, so follow the convention used in the problem.

产生 · carry generate查看释义收起释义

产生 · carry generate

两个操作数都为 111 时，本位一定向高位输出进位 111，与输入进位无关。通常用产生信号 G=ABG=ABG=AB 表示。

AAA bit generates aaa carry when both operands are high. Its carry-out is one independently of carry-in, and the generate signal is commonly written as G=ABG=ABG=AB.

曼彻斯特进位链 · Manchester carry chain查看释义收起释义

曼彻斯特进位链 · Manchester carry chain

将各位的进位产生、消除与传递条件编入串接的开关网络。某位需要传播时，开关把上一级进位接到下一级。可用动态实现减少每位的器件和逻辑级开销，长链仍受串联电阻与电容影响。

AAA Manchester carry chain implements carry generate, kill, and propagate conditions in aaa series switch network. AAA propagating bit connects the incoming carry to the next stage. Dynamic implementations can reduce per-bit logic overhead, while long chains still accumulate resistance and capacitance.

清单来源《DIC 概念题自测》第 2 页

PG 组逻辑 · group propagate–generate logic查看释义收起释义

PG 组逻辑 · group propagate–generate logic

把相邻多位合成一个组，用 PPP 表示组会传入组进位，用 GGG 表示组自身能产生输出进位。若高位组是 HHH、低位组是 LLL，则 P=PHPLP=P_HP_LP=PH​PL​，G=GH+PHGLG=G_H+P_HG_LG=GH​+PH​GL​。这里并写表示逻辑与，加号表示逻辑或。

Group propagate−-−generate logic represents several bits by their combined ability to propagate and generate aaa carry. AAA high group propagates aaa carry from the low group only if its own propagate is active. AAA carry generated by the high group already determines the group output.

跨线 · horizontal wiring tracks查看释义收起释义

跨线 · horizontal wiring tracks

在树加法器中，连接不同位列的信号要横向跨过若干列。跨线数反映某级需要多少条并行的横向布线路径，是比较树结构布线代价的指标。即使两棵树的逻辑级数一样，跨线数也可不同。

Horizontal wiring tracks carry prefix signals across bit columns. Their number measures how many parallel routing lanes are needed at aaa level. Two adders with the same logic depth can require different numbers of tracks.

树加法器 · tree adder (parallel-prefix adder)查看释义收起释义

树加法器 · tree adder (parallel-prefix adder)

用树形网络并行合并相邻位的进位产生与传播信息，得到各位的进位，再求和位。相比逐位等待进位传完，树结构能减少逻辑级数，但可能增加跨线与扇出。

AAA parallel-prefix adder combines carry generate and propagate signals in aaa tree, computes carries in parallel, and then forms the sum bits. It reduces logic depth compared with waiting for aaa carry to ripple through all bits, at aaa possible cost in wiring and fan-out.
