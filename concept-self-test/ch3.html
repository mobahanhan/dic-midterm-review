覆盖电容 · overlap capacitance

栅在源区、漏区边缘有覆盖或边缘电场，形成栅源与栅漏之间的电容。固定工艺下常按单位宽度电容乘器件宽度计算。沟道截止时，这些几何电容仍存在。

Gate overlap and edge fields produce capacitance between the gate and source or drain. In aaa fixed process, it is often modeled as capacitance per unit width times device width. It remains present in cutoff.

沟道电容 · channel capacitance

栅电压变化引起沟道中的电荷变化，由此得到的电容。电荷分到源、漏或体的比例会随工作区改变。进入夹断饱和时，漏侧本征沟道分量常近似为零，覆盖分量仍在。

Channel capacitance comes from changes in channel charge as gate voltage changes. Its partition among terminals depends on the operating region. In pinch-off saturation the intrinsic drain-side component is often approximated as zero, while overlap remains.

结电容 · junction capacitance

PPP 型与 NNN 型半导体接合处，耗尽区中的电荷随偏置改变，表现出电容。反偏增大时，耗尽区通常变宽，结电容减小。MOS\mathrm{MOS}MOS 的漏体结和源体结都可有这种电容。

Junction capacitance is the incremental change of depletion charge with voltage across aPNa \mathrm{PN}aPN junction. Increasing reverse bias usually widens the depletion region and lowers capacitance. MOS\mathrm{MOS}MOS source−-−body and drain−-−body junctions exhibit this effect.

扩散电容 · diffusion capacitance

在二极管语境里，它来自正向导通时注入的少数载流子所储存的电荷。在 MOS\mathrm{MOS}MOS 版图题里，“扩散区电容”指源漏扩散区和体形成的结电容，可分底板和侧壁。看题目是在谈正偏存储电荷，还是扩散区面积与周长。

In aaa diode, diffusion capacitance comes from charge stored by forward injection. In MOS\mathrm{MOS}MOS layout problems, “diffusion capacitance” can refer to the junction capacitance from aaa source or drain diffusion to the body. Area and perimeter data indicate the layout meaning.

源区或漏区底部与体区形成的结电容。把底部面积 A\mathrm{A}A 乘对应偏置下的单位面积结电容 cjc_jcj​，得 Cbottom=AcjC_{bottom}=Ac_jCbottom​=Acj​。

This is the junction capacitance across the bottom of aaa source or drain diffusion. Multiply its bottom area A\mathrm{A}A by the junction capacitance per area cjc_jcj​ at the relevant bias.

侧壁电容 · sidewall junction capacitance

扩散区侧边与周围半导体形成的结电容。侧边长度 PPP 乘对应的单位长度参数 cjswc_{jsw}cjsw​。如果靠栅边与其他边的参数不同，要分开计。

强 0/弱 1 · strong 0 / weak 1

用栅端为高电平的单只 NMOS\mathrm{NMOS}NMOS 当开关，传低电平时输出可接近地。传高电平时输出越高，栅源电压越小，最后在阈值附近停止，输出约为 VG−VTnV_G-V_{\mathrm{Tn}}VG​−VTn​。VGV_GVG​ 是栅压，VTnV_{\mathrm{Tn}}VTn​ 是该偏置下的阈值。

An NMOS\mathrm{NMOS}NMOS pass device with aaa high gate can pull aaa low output close to ground. As it passes aaa high level, its gate-to-source voltage falls until the device approaches cutoff. The high level is limited to roughly the gate voltage minus the NMOS\mathrm{NMOS}NMOS threshold.

体偏效应 · body effect

源极与体区之间的偏压改变耗尽电荷，从而改变开启阈值。对于常见 NMOS\mathrm{NMOS}NMOS，源电位高于体电位使源体结反偏，反偏越强，阈值通常越高。PMOS\mathrm{PMOS}PMOS 用阈值的绝对值比较。

Source-to-body bias changes depletion charge and hence threshold voltage. Increasing reverse source−-−body bias raises the usual NMOS\mathrm{NMOS}NMOS threshold. For PMOS,\mathrm{PMOS} ,PMOS, compare threshold magnitudes.

沟道长度调制 · channel-length modulation

晶体管进入夹断饱和后，继续增大漏源电压，漏端耗尽区向源端扩展，有效沟道变短。于是饱和区的漏电流曲线仍带正斜率。

After pinch-off, increasing drain-to-source voltage extends the drain depletion region and shortens the effective channel. The saturation current therefore still increases with drain voltage.

速度饱和 · velocity saturation

横向电场很强时，载流子的平均漂移速度接近上限。短沟道器件可能在达到长沟道的夹断条件前就进入这种电流饱和，电流对栅压的增长也会偏离平方律。

At high lateral electric field, carrier drift velocity approaches aaa limit. AAA short-channel device can enter current saturation before the long-channel pinch-off condition is reached. Its current then departs from the square-law prediction.

强反型 · strong inversion

栅电压使半导体表面聚集足够多的反型载流子，形成可导电的沟道。对 NMOS\mathrm{NMOS}NMOS，栅源电压明显超过开启阈值时，常使用强反型电流模型。线性区和饱和区都可以处在强反型。

Strong inversion occurs when the gate produces aaa substantial layer of inversion carriers that forms aaa conducting channel. For NMOS,\mathrm{NMOS} ,NMOS, gate-to-source voltage well above threshold is the usual regime. AAA strongly inverted device can be in the linear or saturation region.
