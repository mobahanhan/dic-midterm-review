电压传输特性 · voltage transfer characteristic

固定电源、让电路稳定后，输出电压随输入电压变化的关系。画成曲线时横轴是输入，纵轴是输出，简称 VTC\mathrm{VTC}VTC。反相器的曲线从高输出转向低输出。

The voltage transfer characteristic (VTC)\left(\mathrm{VTC}\right)(VTC) plots the settled output voltage against input voltage at aaa fixed supply. An inverter'sss output moves from high to low as the input rises.

噪声容限 · noise margin

衡量一个门输出的电平还能容许多大的电压扰动，同时让后级读对。若最低高输出、最高低输出分别为 VOHV_{OH}VOH​、VOLV_{OL}VOL​，后级的最低高输入、最高低输入分别为 VIHV_{IH}VIH​、VILV_{IL}VIL​，则 NMH=VOH−VIH\mathrm{NM}_{H}=V_{OH}-V_{IH}NMH​=VOH​−VIH​，NML=VIL−VOL\mathrm{NM}_{L}=V_{IL}-V_{OL}NML​=VIL​−VOL​。

The margin is the allowed voltage disturbance between aaa driver'sss guaranteed output and aaa receiver'sss valid input. For the high level, subtract the minimum valid high input from the minimum guaranteed high output. For the low level, subtract the maximum guaranteed low output from the maximum valid low input.

再生性 · logic-level regeneration

逻辑电平略微偏离标准值时，下一级门能把这种偏差缩小。若电压传输曲线在有效电平附近的斜率绝对值小于 111，小扰动经过该级就会衰减。两级反相器可恢复到同一逻辑极性。

Regeneration is the attenuation of small voltage errors near valid logic levels. AAA local voltage-transfer slope of magnitude below one shrinks such errors, and two inverters restore the original logic polarity.

扇入 · fan-in

一个逻辑门的输入个数。两输入与非门的扇入为 222。输入增加可能增加串联晶体管数或内部负载，影响速度。

Fan-in is the number of inputs of aaa logic gate. AAA two-input NAND has aaa fan-in of two. More inputs can add series transistors or internal loading.

扇出 · fan-out

一个门的输出驱动多少个后级输入。这个数只计连接数。计算延时时常用电气扇出，即总外部负载电容与本级输入电容之比。

Fan-out counts the inputs driven by aaa gate'sss output. For delay calculations, electrical fan-out is the ratio of external load capacitance to the driving gate'sss input capacitance.

Static power is consumed while logic levels are steady. It equals the supply voltage times steady-state current. Leakage is aaa common source, and aaa continuously conducting supply-to-ground path can also contribute.

平均功耗 PavgP_{\mathrm{avg}}Pavg​ 与传播延时 tpt_ptp​ 的乘积，记为 PDP=Pavgtp\mathrm{\mathrm{PDP}}=P_{\mathrm{avg}}t_pPDP=Pavg​tp​，单位为焦耳。把它解释为一次操作的能量时，要先明确功耗对应的活动频率和操作定义。
