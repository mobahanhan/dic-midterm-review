无比逻辑 · ratioless logic

稳态逻辑值由导通路径决定，不依赖两组晶体管互相争流时的尺寸比例。例如静态互补 CMOS\mathrm{CMOS}CMOS 在稳定输入下，输出到电源与到地的两条路径只有一条导通。尺寸比例仍会改变速度和翻转点。

Ratioless logic establishes steady logic levels through complementary conducting paths. In static complementary CMOS,\mathrm{CMOS} ,CMOS, one network connects the output to aaa rail in steady state. Sizing still affects delay and the switching point.

The inverter switching threshold VMV_MVM​ is the point on the transfer characteristic where input equals output. Find it by balancing pull-up and pull-down currents at that voltage. AAA transistor'sss own turn-on threshold is denoted by VTV_TVT​.

在一个输入工作点附近，输出电压对输入电压的局部变化率 g=dVout/dVing=\mathrm{d}V_{out}/\mathrm{d}V_{in}g=dVout​/dVin​。反相器斜率通常为负。转折区越陡，增益绝对值越大。

Inverter voltage gain is the local slope of output voltage versus input voltage. The slope is typically negative, and aaa steeper transition has aaa larger gain magnitude.

密勒效应 · Miller effect

输入和输出之间有电容时，两端同时变化会改变电容的相对电压，因此一端看到的等效负载会改变。等幅反向变化时，相对摆幅为单端的两倍，常把这一端的等效电容近似为原来的两倍。

AAA capacitor between input and output responds to the change in voltage difference. Equal and opposite swings double that difference relative to either swing alone, giving an effective capacitance of about twice its physical value when referred to one node.

自载效应 · self-loading effect

门在驱动外部负载的同时，也必须给自身的输出寄生电容充放电。整门加宽虽降低电阻，却通常增加自身电容。因此即使没有外部负载，门仍有延时。

AAA gate charges and discharges its own output parasitics as well as its external load. Widening the devices generally reduces resistance but increases intrinsic capacitance. AAA gate therefore still has delay without an external load.

反相器短路电流 · inverter short-circuit current

输入翻转时，上拉 PMOS\mathrm{PMOS}PMOS 和下拉 NMOS\mathrm{NMOS}NMOS 可能短暂同时导通，形成电源到地的电流。它的持续时间和大小受输入边沿、供电和输出负载等影响。

During aaa transition, both inverter transistors can conduct briefly, creating direct current from supply to ground. Its duration and magnitude depend on the input edge, supply voltage, and output load.

接触电阻 · contact resistance

电流穿过金属和半导体等材料的接触界面时产生的电阻。它与界面质量和接触面积有关，往往作为额外串联电阻计入导通路径。

Contact resistance is the resistance associated with current crossing aaa material interface, such as metal to semiconductor. It depends on the interface and contact geometry and adds series resistance to the current path.

闩锁效应 · latch-up

CMOS\mathrm{CMOS}CMOS 中井区、体区和扩散区会构成非设计目的的双极晶体管。当它们形成正反馈并被触发时，可能维持一条低电阻的电源到地通路，造成大电流。增加井和体接触、使用保护环可降低风险。

CMOS\mathrm{CMOS}CMOS regions can form unintended bipolar transistors. If triggered into positive feedback, they can sustain aaa low-resistance path from supply to ground. Well and body contacts and guard rings help reduce the risk.
